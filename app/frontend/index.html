🛰️ Satellite EPS Fault Diagnosis — Dynamic-GNN Live Demo

What this page does

This simulates a satellite's electrical power system (solar panel, battery, regulator, power bus, load), optionally breaks something on purpose, then asks four different AI models — live, right now, not precomputed — to guess what's wrong just from the sensor readings. The star of the show is the Dynamic-GNN: instead of a fixed wiring diagram, it re-learns which components matter most to each other at every instant, and you can watch that graph change in real time as the satellite crosses in and out of Earth's shadow.

1Choose a scenario

Each run simulates one full orbit (96 minutes) from scratch using real physics equations (src/simulation/eps_simulator.py) — solar power, battery charge, voltage regulation — then, if you pick a fault, corrupts one of those equations at a random point in time. nominal means nothing is broken; it's deliberately the hardest case to spot.

2Simulated telemetry (this run)

What each of the 5 satellite components reported over the orbit — voltage, current, power, temperature, and (battery only) state-of-charge. Lines are individually rescaled to fit the chart, since volts/amps/watts/°C are different units — compare shapes and timing, not the raw heights between different-colored lines. gray shading = eclipse (no sunlight)   red shading = the fault is actively occurring. Hover any chart for exact values at that moment.

3Live model predictions

All four models look at the exact same telemetry above and independently guess the fault class. The green bar is always the true answer; a model is doing well if its own tallest bar lands on the green one. ⓘThese are live inference calls to the trained PyTorch checkpoints — the bars are each model's softmax confidence across all 5 classes, not a lookup table.

4How good are these models, generally?

Scores on 150 held-out test episodes the models never trained on — the honest measure of how well each one generalizes, not just how it does on this one run above.

ModelParams Acc ⓘAccuracy: % of test episodes classified correctly. Recall ⓘRecall: of the real faults, what fraction did the model actually catch? F1 ⓘF1: balance of recall and precision — punishes both missed faults and false alarms. AUC ⓘAUC: how well-separated the model's confidence scores are between right and wrong classes, 0.5=random, 1.0=perfect.

Validation accuracy during training

Each model's accuracy on unseen validation data, checked after every training pass — shows how fast each learned and where it leveled off. A model that trained well should climb quickly and then hold steady.

5Watch the Dynamic-GNN's graph structure evolve

This is the whole point of "dynamic" in the project name. Instead of assuming SA→PCU→BUS→LOAD is always equally important, the model recomputes — at every single timestep — which components' readings should influence each other most right now. Drag the slider (or hit Play) and watch the wiring visibly shift as the satellite moves from eclipse into sunlight: the battery stops being the dominant power source and the solar array takes over.
